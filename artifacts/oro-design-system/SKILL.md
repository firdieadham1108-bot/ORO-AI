# ORO AI visual usage

Read docs/AGENTS.md and the relevant source-reference family before implementation.
The system is an extraction of ORO AI, not a rebrand.

- Navy is the primary action colour; gold is brand emphasis; warm off-white is the canvas.
- Green communicates available/approved, amber pending/warning, red conflict/declined, blue verification.
- Inter is body/UI, Manrope is headings, IBM Plex Mono is references/times/eyebrows.
- Keep the genuine penguin logo; do not redraw or recolour it.
- Page headers pair compact uppercase eyebrows with strong headings and optional actions.
- Loading and empty results are separate visual states.

These conventions are observed in the source stylesheet and kit; no independent
design documentation existed beyond the app and project brand decisions.
Full dark semantic styles are derived. Do not apply this system to existing screens
without explicit approval.
